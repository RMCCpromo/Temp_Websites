Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

The cost varies based on the size of your home, the scope of work, and paint quality. Interior painting typically ranges from $2,000-$6,000+, while exterior painting ranges from $3,000-$10,000+. We provide free, detailed estimates so there are no surprises.
Most interior projects take 2-5 days, while exterior projects typically take 3-7 days depending on size and weather conditions. We'll give you a precise timeline during the estimate.
We use premium paints from trusted brands like Sherwin-Williams, Benjamin Moore, and Behr. For exteriors, we select products specifically rated for Minnesota's extreme temperature swings and moisture conditions.
The ideal exterior painting season in Minnesota is late spring through early fall (May–October) when temperatures consistently stay above 50°F. We monitor weather closely and schedule accordingly for optimal results.
Absolutely! We provide a written warranty on all projects. Interior work is warrantied for 3 years and exterior work for 5 years. If any issues arise, we'll come back and make it right at no charge.
We ask that you remove small and fragile items, but we'll handle moving larger furniture and protecting everything with clean drop cloths and plastic. We put everything back in its place when we're done!
