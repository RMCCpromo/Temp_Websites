Our Services

Cleaning Services Built Around Your Space

Whether you need a regular cleaning routine, a thorough deep clean, or help getting a property ready, MINNTEX House Cleaning LLC offers flexible options for homes, apartments, condos, and commercial spaces.

Regular House Cleaning

Consistent, top-to-bottom cleaning that keeps your home comfortable week after week — floors, surfaces, dusting, and the everyday spaces you live in.

Deep Cleaning

A detailed, intensive clean that reaches the areas a routine cleaning doesn't — baseboards, vents, edges, buildup, and hard-to-reach spots.

Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning

Start fresh in a new place or leave your old one spotless — a thorough clean of empty or newly emptied homes from room to room.

Apartment & Condo Cleaning

Efficient cleaning for smaller footprints, shared walls, and tight schedules — tailored to apartments, condos, and townhomes.

Kitchen & Bathroom Cleaning

Focused attention on the rooms that need it most — counters, cabinets, sinks, showers, tubs, toilets, tile, and fixtures.

Recurring Cleaning

Weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly visits so your home stays consistently clean without you having to think about it.

Commercial Cleaning

Clean, presentable spaces for offices, retail, rental properties, and small businesses across the Twin Cities Metro Area — scheduled around your hours.
