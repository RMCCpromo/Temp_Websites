About E&M Concrete Services LLC

Based in the heart of the Minnesota metro, E&M Concrete Services delivers unwavering quality and integrity. With over 15 years of experience, our team specializes in decorative stamped concrete, high-strength driveways, and basement finishing — built to endure harsh midwest winters.

We combine modern equipment with old-school craftsmanship, ensuring every pour is flawless. Fully licensed & insured, we’re proud to serve homeowners and businesses across Minneapolis, St. Paul, Bloomington, and surrounding suburbs. Your satisfaction, our foundation.
